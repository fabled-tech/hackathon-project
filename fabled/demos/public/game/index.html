    Start with a pitch

    Fabled Forge turns a game pitch into an asset kit: every character, creature, prop and environment piece becomes a card with a studio import profile (scale, axis, LOD budget, texel density, rig). The team or vendor generates meshes (Meshy, Tripo or the offline mock), pushes them through Fabled Studio's pipeline and pre-import check, lays out the level, and exports a blockout builder for Unreal Engine.

**Research method and caveats.** This session could not fetch any vendor page directly: every outbound WebFetch failed with DNS resolution errors (docs.dev.runwayml.com, developer.adobe.com, autodesk.com, filmustage.com, boords.com, ltx.studio, unrealengine.com, docs.higgsfield.ai, screendaily.com, filmthreat.com, cgpress.org, documentary.org all blocked by the session's network allowlist). All findings below therefore come from WebSearch result summaries, many of them from secondary sources (pricing aggregators, vendor-competitor blogs, trade-press relays). Where a figure is from a reseller/aggregator rather than the vendor it is marked **(secondary)**; where sources disagree both figures are given. Anything older than 2026 carries its date. The search budget ran out before sound/music licensing, Cuebric, Moxion and Pika API could be covered; those are listed as gaps.

## Key question 1: Production pipeline stages and what is automatable today

### Takeaway
As of late 2026 the stages with shipping, widely used AI/automation are: script breakdown and scheduling (Filmustage, Celtx), storyboards/previs (Boords, Katalist, LTX Studio, Previs Pro, AI video models used as "shoestring previs"), editing assistance (DaVinci Resolve 21 AI tools, Premiere Generative Extend), VFX/character replacement (Autodesk Flow Studio), dialogue/lip-sync and dubbing (Flawless DeepEditor, Prime Video AI dubbing), and review/asset tracking (Frame.io V4, Flow Production Tracking, ftrack, Kitsu). Shooting itself, final-pixel generative output for studio work, and rights/clearance remain mostly human-gated by policy (Netflix guidelines, Academy rules, guild contracts) rather than by lack of tooling.

### Cited Findings
- **Pipeline stage: development/writing.** Final Draft 13 was reported at $199.99 one-time, with a "Final Draft Suite" subscription at $16.99/month or $99.99/year, and the same (competitor-authored) source says Final Draft has no AI generation features **(secondary; competitor blog)** — [Argil blog](https://www.argil.ai/blog/ai-movie-script-generator)
- **Script breakdown/scheduling.** Filmustage is described by reviewers as an AI breakdown and scheduling tool with a free "Explorer" tier (AI breakdown, shooting schedule, reports), a paid "Creator" tier adding Final Draft and Excel export, advanced AI budgeting/scheduling in a higher tier, and custom Enterprise pricing; one user review asks for collaborative accounts and another says control after the AI finishes the breakdown could be better **(secondary)** — [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/037096/filmustage); [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/filmustage/reviews); Filmustage positions itself as an alternative to Movie Magic Scheduling — [Filmustage](https://filmustage.com/filmustage-as-an-alternative-to-movie-magic/)
- **Celtx** breakdown/scheduling sits in the Team tier; Team is reported at $44.99/month by one source, $67.49/month (yearly billing, up to 5 members, includes production planning tools) by another, and "custom pricing, base of 5–15 members" by a third; individual Writer plans reported at $11.24–$15/month **(secondary; conflicting)** — [Argil](https://www.argil.ai/blog/ai-movie-script-generator); [ITQlick](https://www.itqlick.com/celtx/pricing); [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/celtx); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/235136/Celtx/)
- **Editing.** DaVinci Resolve 21 final release shipped early June 2026 (public beta announced NAB 2026), free download with Studio at $295; AI additions include IntelliSearch (content search), CineFocus (focal point adjustment) and facial refinement; one review says seven AI features shipped — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/06/03/davinci-resolve-21-officially-released-with-new-photo-editing-ai-tools-and-much-more/); [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/photography/video-editing/photo-editing-grabs-headlines-but-davinci-resolves-big-ai-and-vr-upgrades-will-matter-most-to-its-core-video-users); [dev.to review](https://dev.to/kunal_d6a8fea2309e1571ee7/davinci-resolve-21-ai-features-i-tested-them-on-a-real-project-2026-review-3o81)
- **Premiere Pro Generative Extend** is generally available, uses the Firefly Video Model, extends video in 4K in landscape and vertical and extends audio clips — [Y.M.Cinema](https://ymcinema.com/?p=21921); [BusinessWire, Apr 2 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250402472642/en). A single 2026 source says Firefly Video expanded in early 2026 to scene extension, object removal and AI B-roll **(single source)** — [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/netflixs-ai-gamble-rewriting-hollywoods-playbook-with-generative-tools)
- **VFX / character replacement.** Autodesk Flow Studio (formerly Wonder Studio) is a freemium subscription with monthly render credits; Autodesk cut prices in August 2025 (first free tier, Lite dropped from $20 to $10) — [Autodesk investor PDF](https://investors.autodesk.com/node/35476/pdf); [Digital Production, Aug 13 2025](https://digitalproduction.com/2025/08/13/flow-studio-prices-drop-tiers-multiply-features-shift); [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/tag/autodesk-flow-studio/). A June 2026 listing gives Free / Lite $10 / Standard $45 / Pro $95 per month, Pro bundled in the Media & Entertainment Collection, Wonder 3D (text/image-to-3D) at 20 credits per generation, and 2026 additions of a 3D Editor and node-based Canvas with no price change **(secondary)** — [UsagePricing](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/wonder-dynamics); [UsagePricing Aug 2026 entry](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/wonder-dynamics-2026-08-04-3d-editor-canvas)
- **Dialogue / lip-sync / dubbing.** Flawless DeepEditor moved from beta to commercial availability in February 2025 (announced at the British Film Editors Cut Above Awards); it ships as an extension for Avid Media Composer; its consent system is the Artistic Rights Treasury (A.R.T.), which SAG-AFTRA has endorsed; pricing is enterprise/contact-sales; Flawless reports a #5 ranking in Fast Company's 2026 Most Innovative Companies (Film & TV) — [Broadcast Now](https://broadcastnow.co.uk/production-and-post/flawless-launches-ai-powered-deepeditor/5202005.article); [TNW, Feb 13 2025](https://thenextweb.com/news/filmmaking-ai-startup-flawless-launches-editing-tool); [CineMontage](https://cinemontage.org/?p=62587); [Flawless press page (company-published)](https://flawlessai.com/press/fast-company-2026); [NeuronFeed](https://neuronfeed.com/startups/flawless-ai)
- **Delivery / dubbing at streamer scale.** Amazon announced an AI-aided dubbing pilot on March 5, 2025 (English and Latin American Spanish, 12 licensed titles without existing dubs, "hybrid" AI + human oversight); on September 9, 2026 Prime Video introduced lip-sync technology that matches actors' mouth movements to human-performed dubbed dialogue, debuting on seasons 1–2 of *Maxton Hall* — [About Amazon](https://aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/prime-video-ai-dubbing-english-spanish); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/entertainment/streaming/prime-video-is-beginning-an-ai-dubbing-pilot-program-for-select-movies-and-series-190252188.html); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/prime-video-launches-ai-lip-sync-dubbing-for-maxton-hall/tldr); [IBC](https://ibc.org/artificial-intelligence/news/prime-video-launches-ai-dubbing-pilot-programme/21714)
- **Rights/clearance and provenance.** C2PA Content Credentials are signed metadata recording whether AI was involved, which tool, etc.; YouTube carries forward disclosures from tools with C2PA 2.1+ credentials that indicate a video was entirely AI-made; C2PA's newer AI Disclosure assertion captures model provenance and degree of human oversight; credentials are frequently stripped in platform pipelines ("no platform that The Post tested kept the Content Credentials data") — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15447836?hl=en-uk); [C2PA implementation guide](https://c2pa.org/a-new-implementation-guide-for-content-credentials/); [No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/what-is-c2pa); [Magic Hour guide](https://magichour.ai/blog/content-credentials-c2pa-ai-video)
- The US Copyright Office report reaffirmed that human authorship is essential, that text prompts alone do not confer authorship, and that using AI tools to assist does not undermine copyright in a work — [Pipedot relay](https://pipedot.org/6TXFK); a 2026 guide says the Office continues to follow *Zarya of the Dawn* (arrangement/story protectable, raw AI images often not) **(secondary)** — [AI Video Bootcamp](https://aivideobootcamp.com/blog/ai-disclosure-compliance-2026-c2pa-eu-ai-act/)

### Inferences
- The stages with the most mature automation are the ones that are text-in/structured-data-out (breakdown, scheduling, shot lists) or that operate on already-shot pixels (extend, relight, lip-sync, dubbing). Generating new final-pixel footage for a studio release remains gated by policy (see Q5), not by model availability.
- Rights/clearance has no end-to-end automation product in the results; what exists is provenance metadata (C2PA) plus policy. A tool that attaches C2PA credentials at each pipeline hand-off and survives export would address a documented gap.

### Gaps
- No vendor pricing page for Filmustage, Celtx, Final Draft or Flow Studio could be read directly; all figures are secondary and conflicting.
- Color grading and sound/music AI (ElevenLabs, Suno, Adobe sound tools) were not researched because the search budget was exhausted. Treat these as unverified.
- No source quantified how much of a breakdown Filmustage gets right, or any accuracy benchmark for script-breakdown AI.

## Key question 2: Script-to-storyboard / script-to-previs / shot-list tools

### Takeaway
The category is crowded but fragmented: Boords (approval-oriented storyboards), Katalist (script-to-consistent-storyboard, ~$19–39/mo), LTX Studio (script-to-animatic with commercial use from ~$35/mo), Saga (script+storyboard+previs video), Previs Pro (3D previs app), Storyboarder (free, manual) and Filmustage/Celtx (breakdown-side) each cover one slice. None of the results documented a tool that exports a shot list or storyboard directly into Unreal Sequencer.

### Cited Findings
- **LTX Studio** plans reported as Free (8,000 computing seconds), Lite $15/mo, Standard $35/mo (adds commercial use), Pro $125/mo by one guide; another roundup says plans start at $19/mo **(secondary; conflicting)** — [ai-storyboard.com comparison](https://ai-storyboard.com/blog/posts/ai-storyboard-tools-comparison-2026); [Higgsfield previs roundup (vendor blog)](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-previsualization-tools)
- **Katalist AI**: generative tool to turn scripts into detailed, character-consistent storyboards for filmmakers and advertisers; pricing reported as free tier with 50 credits (no export) and Essential from $19/mo by one source, $29/mo for 50 boards by another, starting at $39/mo by a third; markets an "agentic storyboarder" — [Katalist agentic storyboarder page](https://www.katalist.ai/agentic-storyboarder); [FutureTools](https://www.futuretools.io/tools/katalist); [topai.tools](https://topai.tools/alternatives/katalist-ai); [Domo AI roundup](https://domoai.app/blog/ai-storyboard-generators-2026). Preqin reports KatalistAI, Inc. raised USD 180,000 seed led by Orbit Ventures in March 2026 — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/katalistai--inc-/782775)
- **Boords**: only firm price found is a G2 listing of a "Lite" tier at $19.00/month for freelancers; comparison pages call it strongest for client approvals and fine panel control; tiered pricing otherwise unspecified **(secondary)** — [G2 (Boords via Story Planner listing)](https://www.g2.com/products/story-planner-for-writers/pricing); [MockFlow roundup](https://mockflow.com/blog/best-ai-storyboard-generators)
- **Saga**: positioned as all-in-one AI filmmaking (writes scripts, creates storyboards, generates previs videos), reported as powered by GPT-4o and Google Veo 3; freemium, no plan prices found **(secondary)** — [ToolMage Nolan vs Saga](https://www.toolmage.com/en/compare/nolanai-vs-saga-1/)
- **Nolan (FinalBit)**: AI screenwriting and pre-production platform covering script coverage, automated storyboarding, budgeting and scheduling; pricing not published per directories **(secondary)** — [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/nolan); [FinalBit blog](https://finalbitai.com/blog/democratizing-screenwriting)
- **Storyboarder (Wonder Unit)** is a free desktop tool, manual drawing only; **Storyboarder.ai** is a different, AI product that generates panels from PDF/Fountain uploads or prompts, freemium with a full platform reported from $66/mo **(secondary)** — [ai-storyboard.com](https://ai-storyboard.com/blog/posts/ai-storyboard-tools-comparison-2026)
- **Previs Pro** added animatics, lighting tools and a free tier (CGPress article; content could not be fetched, date unverified) — [CGPress](https://cgpress.org/archives/previs-pro-adds-animatics-lighting-tools-and-free-tier.html)
- **Cine Tracer** is a real-time cinematography simulator built by Matt Workman, originating as a Cinema 4D previs plug-in, now with set building and digital humans (results dated 2020–2021; current price not found) — [SIGGRAPH Asia 2020 session](https://sa2020.siggraph.org/en/attend/games/session/244)
- **AI video as previs.** Film Threat (Oct 2026) reports indie directors generating AI previs clips themselves before the first shoot day, noting previs/storyboard artists on low-budget productions "were often the first people hired and the cheapest line in the budget" and that some of that work is now absorbed by directors; a Sept 2026 piece says most AI previs tools work from a written description rather than 3D skills — [Film Threat, Oct 2026](https://filmthreat.com/features/previs-on-a-shoestring-how-indie-directors-are-using-ai-video-before-the-first-shoot-day/); [Film Threat, Sep 2026](https://filmthreat.com/features/ai-previsualization-test-your-film-before-final-production-costs/)
- **Flow Studio** (Autodesk) also offers Wonder 3D text/image-to-3D at 20 credits per generation (March 2026 announcement, relayed) — [UsagePricing Mar 2026 entry](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/wonder-dynamics-2026-03-05-wonder-3d-credits)

### Inferences
- The script-to-storyboard tools are consumer-priced ($15–$125/mo) and image-model-based; the 3D previs tools (Previs Pro, Cine Tracer, Unreal) are a separate world with no documented bridge from the AI storyboard tools. A shot list → 3D blocking → Unreal Sequencer path is the clearest missing link in the results.
- Team collaboration (comments, roles, versions) is a selling point only for Boords (client approvals) among the storyboard tools found; Filmustage reviewers explicitly ask for collaborative accounts.

### Gaps
- No official pricing page could be read for Boords, Katalist, LTX Studio, Saga, Nolan, Previs Pro or Cine Tracer. Figures above are from aggregators and conflict.
- No source documented API availability for any storyboard/previs tool.
- No source documented Unreal Engine export (FBX/USD/Sequencer) for Previs Pro, Katalist, LTX Studio or Boords. Unverified.

## Key question 3: AI video generation products used in film workflows; APIs and commercial licences

### Takeaway
By November 2026 the API-accessible generators are Runway (Gen-4.5, Gen-4 Turbo, Aleph 2.0), Google Veo 3.1 (Standard/Fast/Lite), Kling 3.0 (official Kuaishou API), MiniMax Hailuo (02 / 2.3 / H3), Luma Ray (details thin), and Adobe Firefly Video (inside Premiere; API status conflicting). OpenAI's Sora 2 API was removed on September 24, 2026 and Google's Veo 3 API was shut down June 30, 2026. Commercial-use rights are generally tied to paid tiers and are not spelled out for aggregators like Higgsfield.

## Key question 4: Collaboration/review platforms and their APIs

### Takeaway
Frame.io (Adobe) dominates review for editorial, with a V4 REST API (OAuth 2.0 via Adobe Developer Console, webhooks on comments/files/version stacks, custom actions in beta); Autodesk Flow Production Tracking (ex-ShotGrid) and Backlight's ftrack Studio own VFX/animation tracking with Python APIs and webhooks; Kitsu (CGWire) is the open-source self-hostable option. Pricing: Frame.io Free / Pro ~$15 / Team ~$25 per member/month; Flow PT reported $50/user/month or $390/user/year; ftrack ~$25/user/month.

### Cited Findings
- **Frame.io V4 API:** webhooks are push HTTP callbacks subscribable by category — Projects, Files, Folders, Comments, Metadata, Collections, Custom Fields, Shares; pre-migration webhooks are disabled (`is_active` field); team ID moved from payload to URL path; V4 endpoints require OAuth 2.0 tokens from the Adobe Developer Console (legacy developer tokens/JWTs rejected); custom actions are in beta, trigger on files, folders and version stacks, and pre-V4 custom actions are nulled; version stacks are a V4 resource listed among in-progress features — [Frame.io V4 webhooks guide](https://next.developer.frame.io/platform/docs/guides/webhooks); [Frame.io custom actions guide](https://next.developer.frame.io/fr-FR/platform/v4/docs/guides/custom-actions.md); [Frame.io V4 API release notes (forum)](https://forum.frame.io/t/frame-io-v4-api-release-notes/2527); [V4 getting started](https://next.developer.frame.io/platform/docs/getting-started)
- **Frame.io pricing (Oct 2026 reads, secondary):** Free, Pro $15/member/month monthly ($156/yr), Team $25/member/month ($264/yr), Enterprise custom; Free listed as up to 2 members, 2 GB, 2 projects; Pro up to 5 members, 2 TB; Camera to Cloud included (tier placement ambiguous) — [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/frame-io/pricing); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/frame); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/148214/Frame-io/pricing/). ## Key question 6: 2025–2026 industry developments (unions, studios, festivals/distribution)

The Academy's May 2026 rules make AI performances and AI-written scripts ineligible for acting/writing awards while allowing AI tools generally. Studios are adopting in bounded ways: Netflix's August 2025 partner guidelines allow pre-production/temp GenAI but require approval for final on-screen content; Lionsgate took an equity stake in Runway (June 11, 2026) after the 2024 custom-model deal reportedly stalled; Prime Video added AI lip-sync dubbing (Sept 2026). Tribeca 2026 screened a $2,000 fully AI-generated feature.

## Key question 7: Where indie filmmakers and creators say the pain is

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and mostly anecdotal: the only survey found is IDA's 2025 documentary survey (146 respondents; 74% plan to use GenAI, top concern inaccurate representation 4.1/5, then audience trust 4.0, labor 3.9; 64% use it for proposals/applications). No 2026 quantitative survey of indie narrative filmmakers' budgets, coordination or versioning pain was found.

### Cited Findings
- IDA/Google/Sundance Institute preliminary survey (June 2025) of independent documentary filmmakers: 26% do not plan to use GenAI in their films (≈74% do); concern ratings on 1–5: inaccurate representations 4.1, eroding audience trust 4.0, labor 3.9; strongest use in research & development, especially writing proposals/applications (64%); respondents 59% director/producer, 21% director, 20% producer; 146 respondents per IDA's announcement — [IDA preliminary findings PDF](https://www.documentary.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/%5BPreliminary%20Findings%5D%20Filmmaker%20Survey_%20Generative%20AI%20and%20Documentary%20Film%20.pdf); [IDA Core App 3.1 addendum](https://documentary.org/index%2ephp/blog/core-app-31-artificial-intelligence-addendum)

# Research: film and game creative-tool products (November 2026)

- **Report:** [`film-and-game-creative-tool-products.md`](film-and-game-creative-tool-products.md). Answers: what should Fabled build next, given a business model of industry-specific collaborative creative tools that plug into Unreal Engine, Higgsfield and similar? Ends with a prioritized product table (film line and game line) and a verification ledger.
- **Notes** (one file per research thread, every claim inline-sourced, each section with Takeaway / Cited findings / Inferences / Gaps):
  - `notes/film_landscape.md`: production stages, shipping tools and prices, Unreal in previs and virtual production, 2026 union and studio policy, indie pain points.
  - `notes/game_landscape.md`: 3D generation APIs (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Hunyuan3D, TRELLIS), the generated-mesh-to-engine gap, animation, NPCs, Epic's UE 5.8 / UE6 direction, market stats.
  - `notes/unreal_integration.md`: editor Python, Interchange, Sequencer and Movie Render Graph scripting, Remote Control, the 5.8 Unreal MCP plugin, Multi-User, Fab, MetaHuman, EULA and seat pricing.
  - `notes/higgsfield_and_video_apis.md`: Higgsfield product, API (base URL, `Authorization: Key` auth, endpoints, polling), pricing, terms, company facts; comparison with Runway, Luma, Kling, Veo 3.1, MiniMax, fal, Replicate.
  - `notes/business_model_and_market.md`: comparables and funding, pricing patterns, unit costs and margins, shutdowns, legal and platform rules, demand evidence.

**Evidence caveat.** Most vendor and press sites were unreachable from the research environment, so the majority of prices and endpoints come from search-engine extracts of official pages and third-party trackers. The report's last section grades every fact (primary source, official page via snippet, or secondary) and lists the open questions. Re-verify any endpoint or price against live documentation before coding or quoting it.

The two demos built from this research live in [`fabled/demos/`](../../fabled/demos/README.md).
